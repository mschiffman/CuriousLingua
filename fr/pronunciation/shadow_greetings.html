Shadowing: greetings

Saluer et prendre congé

Short phrases to start with. Match the melody: French greetings rise at the end.

Three passes

Play it through. Follow the words with your eyes.

Speak along with the voice, a split second behind it. Copy the rhythm before the words.

Same again, from your ear only. Loop any chunk that trips you.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Listen for

  • The last syllable carries the stress: bon-JOUR, bon-SOIR, sa-LUT.
  • The r in bonjour and bonsoir is soft, at the back of the throat.
  • Final consonants are silent: salut, comment.
