Le futur proche

Near future: aller + infinitive

Describes immediate or near future actions. Very common in everyday speech. Equivalent to English “going to do something”.

When to use itFormationSignal wordsNegation — ne … pas / jamais / plusQuestions — intonation / est-ce que / inversionPrésent vs. Futur proche

When to use it

Planned action

something you intend to do

  • Je vais appeler ma sœur.

    I'm going to call my sister.

  • Il va commencer demain.

    He's going to start tomorrow.

  • On va faire les courses.

    We're going grocery shopping.

  • Je vais voir ce qu'il me reste.

    I'll see what I have left.

  • Nous allons changer ça.

    We are going to change that.

Immediate future

about to happen very soon

  • Attention, ça va tomber!

    Watch out, it's going to fall!

  • Je vais vomir...

    I'm going to throw up...

  • Le train va partir.

    The train is about to leave.

Prediction

based on current evidence

  • Il va pleuvoir.

    It's going to rain.

  • Elle va dire non, je le sens.

    She's going to say no, I can feel it.

  • Ça va être compliqué.

    That's going to be complicated.

Uncertainty / Possibility

predicting or wondering about what might happen

  • Je sais pas si ça va marcher.

    I don't know if it's going to work.

  • Tu crois que ça va suffire?

    Do you think it's going to be enough?

  • Je me demande si ça va aller.

    I wonder if it’s going to be okay.

  • On verra comment ça va se passer.

    We’ll see how it’s going to go.

informal scheduling

When talking about upcoming events

  • On va manger chez eux samedi.

    We’re going to eat at their place Saturday.

  • Je vais travailler demain.

    I'm going to work tomorrow.

  • Tu vas venir avec nous ?

    Are you going to come with us?

Formation

Structure

subject + aller (present) + infinitive

  • Je vais partir.

    I'm going to leave.

  • Tu vas comprendre.

    You're going to understand.

  • Il va pleuvoir.

    It's going to rain.

  • Nous allons manger.

    We’re going to eat.

  • Vous allez voir.

    You're going to see.

  • Ils vont venir.

    They're going to come.

Notes

  • This is the spoken future in French — more natural than futur simple in everyday talk.
  • Works with reflexive verbs: Je vais me lever à 7h.
  • Negative: Place ne and pas around aller
    → Je ne vais pas sortir.

Signal words

ce soir, demain, bientôt, tout à l’heure, dans une minute, la semaine prochaine, ce week-end, dans un instant, d’ici peu …

Negation — ne … pas / jamais / plus

  • Je ne vais pas venir.

    I'm not going to come.

  • Il ne va jamais changer.

    He's never going to change.

  • On ne va plus sortir avec eux.

    We're no longer going to go out with them.

Questions — intonation / est-ce que / inversion

  • Tu vas venir?

    Are you going to come?

  • Est-ce que vous allez sortir?

    Are you going to go out?

  • Va-t-il pleuvoir ?

    Is it going to rain?

Présent vs. Futur proche

  • Présent: action in progress — Je mange.

    I'm eating.

  • Futur proche: imminent or scheduled — Je vais manger.

    I'm going to eat.
