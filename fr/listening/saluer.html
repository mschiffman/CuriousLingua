Saluer

Saying hello with tu and with vous

Two short scenes. Léa bumps into her friend Tom and says tu; then she meets a new neighbour, Madame Martin, and says vous. Listen for how the greetings change.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Salut ! (tu) = Hi! Bonjour ! (tu and vous) = Hello! Use salut only with people you say tu to.
  • Ça va ? (tu) = How's it going? Comment allez-vous ? (vous) = How are you? Answer: Ça va bien, merci. Et toi ? / Et vous ?
  • Je m'appelle Léa = My name is Léa. Moi, c'est Madame Martin = I'm Madame Martin (more relaxed).
  • Enchanté(e) = Nice to meet you. A woman writes enchantée with an extra e; it sounds the same.
  • À plus tard ! = See you later! À demain ! = See you tomorrow! Au revoir ! = Goodbye!
  • Bonne journée ! = Have a good day! The answer: Toi aussi. (tu) / Vous aussi. (vous) = You too.
  • Bonjour, madame: in French, you add madame or monsieur to be polite, with no name after it.
