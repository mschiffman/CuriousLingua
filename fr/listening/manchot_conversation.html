Le manchot empereur : un papa extraordinaire

Camille raconte l’hiver du papa manchot

Camille tells Thomas how emperor penguins raise a chick in the Antarctic winter, month by month, from April to December. It goes with the reading text « Le manchot qui défie l’hiver » and brings its words back in conversation. About five minutes, at natural speed.

How to use this lesson

  1. Listen a first time without looking at the text. Try to follow the stages of the adventure, from April to December.
  2. Listen again and answer the questions under the dialogue.
  3. Then listen with the text showing, and pick out the familiar expressions Camille and Thomas use.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • Figure-toi que… = would you believe…, get this. Tu ne crois pas si bien dire = you don’t know how right you are.
  • il a intérêt à + infinitive = he had better…. Ce n’est pas une promenade de santé = it’s no walk in the park.
  • Et dire que… = and to think that…. Rien que d’en parler = just talking about it; rien qu’à sa voix = by his voice alone.
  • le père, lui, … / eux, ils… : the extra pronoun marks a contrast (she can eat; he, on the other hand, cannot).
