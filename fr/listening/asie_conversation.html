L’Asie : un continent ou un monde à part ?

Camille et Thomas font le tour du continent

Two friends talk their way across Asia, from Siberia to Japan. It goes with the reading text « L’Asie, franchement, c’est énorme » and brings its words back in conversation. About five minutes, at natural speed.

How to use this lesson

  1. Read the text « L’Asie, franchement, c’est énorme » first if you have not yet.
  2. Press Play all with the text hidden. Listen for the gist: which places do they talk about, and in what order?
  3. Listen again, then answer the questions under the dialogue. Show the text only to check what you could not catch.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • t’as = tu as. pas mal = quite a lot (pas mal de monde, ça bouge pas mal). un truc = une chose.
  • Spoken fillers to notice: dis, tiens, remarque, justement, voilà, d’ailleurs, bref, hein.
  • ça cogne = the sun is beating down. un froid de canard = bitter cold. Both are familiar: fine with friends, not in writing.
  • l’emporter sur = to win out over. On ne se refait pas = you can’t change who you are.
