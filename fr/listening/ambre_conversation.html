L’ambre : un trésor du passé

Camille découvre d’où vient l’ambre

Camille sees a photo of a golden stone, and Thomas tells her where it really comes from. It goes with the reading text « L’ambre » and brings its words back in conversation. About three minutes.

How to use this lesson

  1. Listen a first time without reading the dialogue, and try to answer the questions.
  2. Then listen a second time with the text in front of you.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • Reacting with surprise: Ah bon ? Quoi ? Tu plaisantes ! Incroyable !
  • on dirait = it looks like. tel qu’il était / telle qu’elle était = just as it was.
  • devenir + noun or adjective: la résine devient de l’ambre, l’ambre devient bleu.
