Le cerveau et les langues : une histoire d’amour

Camille veut apprendre le japonais

Camille and Thomas talk about the brain: what it manages, how its parts work together, and what happens in it when you learn a language. It goes with the reading text « Le cerveau et les langues » and brings its words back in conversation. About three minutes.

How to use this lesson

  1. Listen a first time without reading the dialogue, and try to answer the questions.
  2. Then listen a second time with the text in front of you.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • 2,5 millions is said « deux virgule cinq millions ». une livre = a pound (about half a kilo); un livre = a book.
  • Being careful with facts: on dit que…, les études montrent que…, certaines études parlent de…, j’ai entendu dire que….
  • le cervelet, lui, … = the cerebellum, for its part: lui / elle after a noun marks a contrast with what came before.
