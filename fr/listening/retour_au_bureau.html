Retour au bureau

Back to the office

Two colleagues react to an email from management: four days a week in the office from January. Natural spoken French, at normal speed.

How to use this lesson

  1. Press Play all with the text hidden. Listen for the gist only: what is the news, and how does each person feel about it?
  2. Listen again, then answer the questions under the dialogue. The full quiz is in the Supplemental section at the bottom.
  3. Tap the lines to show the text, or switch off Hide text. Listen once more while reading.
  4. Shadow it: play a line, press Pause (or the space bar), repeat it aloud, then Resume.
  5. Read the notes on spoken French, then take a role and say the lines in the pauses.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • The ne disappears in speech: j'arrive pas, j'ai jamais été, c'est pas rien, tu trouves pas, ça m'étonnerait pas.
  • t'as = tu as. ouais = oui. bosser = travailler. plein de trucs = beaucoup de choses.
  • ça m'a refroidi: it cooled my enthusiasm. ça m'arrange: it suits me.
  • décrocher: literally 'to unhook'; here, to switch off from work.
  • n'empêche que…: 'all the same', 'even so'. It concedes the point, then pushes back.
  • de là à + infinitive: 'but to go as far as…'. Julien leaves the sentence open, which is very common in speech.
  • mettre quelqu'un devant le fait accompli: to present someone with a decision already made.
  • je doute que ça ait changé: douter que takes the subjunctive (here, the past subjunctive).
  • Si, c'est vrai: si, not oui, because Julien is answering a negative question (tu trouves pas… ?).
  • histoire de + infinitive: 'just so as to'. y laisser sa santé: to ruin your health over it.
  • ça se défend: 'that's a defensible view'. Tu parles !: ironic, 'yeah, right!'.
  • le râleur de service: the resident moaner. 'de service' marks someone who always plays the same part.
