Le garde-manger et les cartons

Sorting the pantry before a move

The painters are in the house and a move is coming. Élise and Thomas empty the pantry and decide what to keep. Everyday spoken French, at normal speed.

How to use this lesson

  1. Press Play all with the text hidden. Listen for the gist only: what are they doing, and what do they decide?
  2. Listen again, then answer the questions under the dialogue.
  3. Tap the lines to show the text, or switch off Hide text. Listen once more while reading.
  4. Shadow it: play a line, press Pause (or the space bar), repeat it aloud, then Resume.
  5. Read the notes on spoken French, then take a role and say the lines in the pauses.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • des trucs = des choses. le boulot = le travail. boucler = finir. Ça marche = d'accord.
  • Pointing with ça and repeating it: Et ça, je le mets où ? / Tu t'en sers, de ça ? Spoken French names the thing first or last and uses a pronoun in the middle.
  • une fois + past participle: une fois le revêtement posé = once the liner is down. No verb needed.
  • quand + futur antérieur for something finished before another future action: quand les peintres auront enlevé la bâche, les caisses iront par terre.
  • Et si + imparfait makes a suggestion: Et si je te les apportais ? = What if I brought them to you?
  • on ferait mieux de + infinitive = we'd be better off ...; with ne rien: on ferait mieux de ne rien remettre.
  • faire passer quelqu'un pour = to make someone look like: ne me fais pas passer pour la méchante.
