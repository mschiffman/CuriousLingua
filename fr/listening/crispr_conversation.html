CRISPR : les ciseaux qui réécrivent la vie

Thomas explique CRISPR à Camille

Camille has read that we can now rewrite our DNA, and Thomas explains how: a giant book, a typo, and a trick borrowed from bacteria. It goes with the reading text « CRISPR » and brings its words back in conversation. About three minutes.

How to use this lesson

  1. Listen a first time without reading the dialogue, and try to answer the questions.
  2. Then listen a second time with the text in front of you.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • l’ADN is said letter by letter: « a-dé-enne ». CRISPR is said « crispeur ».
  • c’est une chose…, c’en est une autre = … is one thing, … is quite another.
  • Comme quoi, … = which just goes to show that…. It opens a conclusion drawn from what was just said.
