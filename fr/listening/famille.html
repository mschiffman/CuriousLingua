La famille

Looking at family photos

Emma and Lucas again. Emma shows Lucas the family photos on her phone, then Lucas shows her one of his. Listen for who is who: names, ages and jobs.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • C'est qui ? = Who's that? In everyday speech qui goes last. C'est qui, sur la photo ? = Who's that in the photo?
  • C'est mon père. C'est ma sœur. Ce sont mes cousins. c'est for one person, ce sont for more than one.
  • mon père, ma mère, mes parents = my. ton frère, ta famille, tes parents = your (tu). mon, ma or mes goes with the word that follows, not with the person speaking: Lucas also says ma mère.
  • Ils s'appellent comment ? = What are their names? Il s'appelle Marc. Elle s'appelle Claire.
  • Age, with avoir: Il a vingt-cinq ans. Elle a seize ans.
  • Et lui ? Et elle ? = And him? And her? A quick way to point to someone in a photo.
  • Tu as des frères et sœurs ? = Do you have any brothers and sisters? Je suis fils unique (a boy) / fille unique (a girl) = I'm an only child.
  • les enfants de ma tante = my aunt's children. French has no 's: it uses de.
  • le lycée = the last three years of school, from about 15 to 18. cuisinier (a man) / cuisinière (a woman) = a cook.
  • Elle a l'air très gentille = She looks very kind. avoir l'air + an adjective = to look, to seem.
