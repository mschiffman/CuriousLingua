Les Vikings : à la découverte du monde

Camille et Thomas imaginent la vie des Vikings

Would you have liked to live in the time of the Vikings? Camille and Thomas talk about their ships, their life at home and how far they sailed. It goes with the reading text « Les Vikings, explorateurs des mers » and brings its words back in conversation. About three minutes.

How to use this lesson

  1. Listen a first time without reading the dialogue, and try to answer the questions.
  2. Then listen a second time with the text in front of you.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • tu aurais aimé…, j’aurais adoré… = you would have liked…, I would have loved…: the past conditional, for imagining a past that did not happen.
  • The imparfait describes how things used to be: ils partaient, elles cultivaient, ils racontaient. The passé composé gives what they achieved: ils se sont installés, ils sont allés.
  • assez rapides pour… mais assez plats pour… = fast enough to… but flat enough to….
