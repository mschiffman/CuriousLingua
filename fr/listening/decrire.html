Décrire quelqu'un

Meeting someone at the station

Emma's brother and sister, Thomas and Manon from La famille, are arriving in Lyon, and Lucas goes to meet them at the station. Emma tells him what they look like and what they're like. Listen first with the text hidden. Then show it and listen again.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Ils sont comment ? = What are they like? What do they look like? Also Il est comment ? Elle est comment ? The answer can be about looks or about character.
  • Most adjectives add an e for a woman: grand / grande, petit / petite, gentil / gentille. Those that already end in e stay the same: mince, drôle, timide. sympa never changes.
  • Hair and eyes take avoir and les: Il a les cheveux bruns et courts. Elle a les yeux bleus. The colour and the length come after cheveux. marron never changes: les yeux marron.
  • porter = to wear: Elle porte des lunettes. A beard is had, not worn: Il a une barbe.
  • Colours come after the noun: une valise rouge, un sac noir. A few short adjectives come before it: un grand sac, une petite fille.
  • sympa = friendly, nice. drôle = funny. timide = shy. gentil / gentille = kind.
  • comme moi = like me. Tu es super ! = You're great!
