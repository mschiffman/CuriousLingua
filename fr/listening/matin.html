Le matin

A late breakfast at home

It's ten o'clock and the child has only just got up. Listen first with the text hidden. Then show it and listen again.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • la marmotte = the marmot, an animal that sleeps all winter. A teasing name for someone who sleeps late.
  • Tu veux quoi ? Il est où, papa ? The question word goes last: normal in spoken French.
  • Je vais te faire un jus, il va t'emmener, je vais m'habiller: aller + infinitive talks about the near future.
  • Je n'ai plus faim = I'm not hungry any more. ne ... plus = no longer.
  • pain perdu = French toast (literally 'lost bread': stale bread saved with egg and milk). œufs au plat = fried eggs.
  • chez le coiffeur = to the hairdresser's. chez + a person = at or to their place.
