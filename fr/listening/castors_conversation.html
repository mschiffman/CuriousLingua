Les castors : les petits architectes de la nature

Camille raconte ce que font les castors

Camille tells Thomas why beavers are little geniuses: their teeth, their dams, their ponds and what they leave behind. It goes with the reading text « Les castors » and brings its words back in conversation. About three minutes.

How to use this lesson

  1. Listen a first time without reading the dialogue, and try to answer the questions.
  2. Then listen a second time with the text in front of you.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • bosser = travailler, un bosseur = someone who works hard. Familiar: fine with friends, not in writing.
  • Asking for more: Ah bon ? Comment ça ? Comme quoi ? Lesquels, par exemple ? À quoi servent… ?
  • on dirait… = you’d think…, it looks like…. ça donne envie de + infinitive = it makes you want to….
