Merci !

Four ways to say thank you

A present, a bakery, a dinner, a stranger in the street: four short scenes, and a different thank-you in each. Listen for who says tu and who says vous.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • With friends: merci, merci beaucoup, and the answers de rien or avec plaisir.
  • With strangers: je vous remercie is more formal than merci. The polite answer is je vous en prie (with a friend: je t'en prie).
  • C'est tout, merci answers « Et avec ceci ? » in a shop: 'That's all, thanks.'
  • Merci à vous returns the thanks: 'No, thank YOU.'
  • Merci pour tout closes a visit or a long favour. Encore merci is for when you have already said thank you once.
