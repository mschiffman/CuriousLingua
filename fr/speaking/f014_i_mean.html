Je veux dire…

Four ways to explain yourself

A suspicious cake, a shrunken jumper, a plant that is 'resting' and a sofa with a spring sticking out: four short scenes between Emma and Lucas. Listen for the little phrases they use to correct themselves, explain again and play for time.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Je veux dire and enfin correct what you just said. Ou plutôt swaps in a better word. En fait gives the real version.
  • C'est-à-dire ? asks for an explanation; c'est-à-dire que… gives one. Autrement dit draws the conclusion.
  • Comment dire…, disons que… and en quelque sorte buy time, or soften bad news.
  • Genre, après and cela dit are spoken qualifiers: 'like', 'mind you', 'that said'.
  • Si says yes to a negative: Tu n'aimes pas. — Si !
  • Spoken French drops ne and shortens il y a: je sais pas, on peut plus, y a.
