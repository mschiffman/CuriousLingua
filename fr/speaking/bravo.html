Bravo !

Four ways to say well done

A light bulb changed after three months, a driving test passed at the fifth attempt, a pancake that does not come back down and a new job that has to be celebrated: four short scenes between Emma and Lucas. Listen for how they congratulate each other, and for the moments when the praise is not entirely sincere.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Bravo and bien joué praise something a person did. Félicitations is for a success or a life event: an exam, a job, a wedding.
  • Chapeau and toutes mes félicitations are strong. Used for a small thing, they tease.
  • Tu l'as bien mérité and tous tes efforts ont payé praise the work behind the result.
  • T'as assuré, t'as géré, t'as déchiré and trop fort are slang, for friends. T'as = tu as.
  • Fier, content for a man; fière, contente for a woman: je suis fier de toi, tu peux être fière de toi.
  • Ça se fête ! proposes a celebration. If you say je t'invite, you are paying.
