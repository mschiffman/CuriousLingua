Dans la rue

Six very short exchanges at a crossroads

A pedestrian, a driver, a cyclist. Each exchange is three or four lines: ask, warn, apologise, thank.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Allez-y (vous) and Vas-y (tu) both mean “go ahead”.
  • Pas de problème, pas de souci, ce n'est rien: three everyday ways to say “that's all right”.
  • Excusez-moi (vous) becomes excuse-moi (tu). The driver and the cyclist say tu to each other in the last two exchanges.
