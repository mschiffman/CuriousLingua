Au marché

Four short exchanges at the stalls

Vegetables, bread, cheese, fish: the same few moves each time. Greet, ask, say how much, say thank you.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • J’en prends un kilo: en stands for « des tomates ». It goes before the verb.
  • Elle est du jour ? = was it baked today?
  • With a stallholder you know, the same exchanges go into tu: see the informal dialogues in the vocabulary lesson's exercises.
