Il est quelle heure ?

Four conversations about the time

A stranger in the street, a film with a friend, a late morning, a train to catch: four short scenes, and a different way of saying the time in each. Listen for the twelve-hour clock between friends and the twenty-four-hour clock at the station.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Il est quelle heure ? is the everyday question. Quelle heure est-il ? is the careful one. To a stranger: vous avez l'heure, s'il vous plaît ?
  • Il est + the time: il est dix heures et quart. À + the time says when: le film commence à huit heures et demie.
  • Et quart (:15), et demie (:30), moins le quart (:45), moins dix (:50). With moins you name the hour that is coming.
  • Stations, timetables and appointments use the twenty-four-hour clock, with the minutes as a plain number: quatorze heures trente-cinq.
  • … à quelle heure ? at the end of the sentence is the spoken order: le film commence à quelle heure ? More careful: à quelle heure commence le film ?
  • Heure(s) is never left out in French: il est neuf heures, not « il est neuf ».
