The family tree: who is who, and for whom

A family word depends on who is speaking: the same woman is ma mère, ma fille or ma tante. This is the family of Emma, from the listening lesson La famille. Choose who « moi » is and watch every word change. Move the slider to bring in more of the family.

Notes

  • Blue words are masculine (mon), pink words are feminine (ma).
  • Before a vowel sound, ma becomes mon: mon arrière-grand-mère, although the word is feminine.
  • Le beau-père and la belle-mère are also the words for a stepfather and a stepmother. La belle-fille is a daughter-in-law or a stepdaughter.
  • La femme is both the woman and the wife. Le fils /fis/: the l is silent and the s is heard. La fille is both the girl and the daughter.
  • When a card shows no word, French has no single word for that link: people say it in full, like « le cousin de mon père ».
