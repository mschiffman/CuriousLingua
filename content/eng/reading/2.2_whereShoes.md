# Where Are My Shoes?

## Slide 1

I’m almost ready.
I have my bag.
But where are my shoes?

## Slide 2

Where are my shoes?
I can’t find them.
I look everywhere.

## Slide 3

Are they under the bed?
I look under the bed.
No, they’re not.

## Slide 4

Are they in the closet?
I look in the closet.
No shoes!

## Slide 5

Mom, I can’t find my shoes!
Let’s look together.
Are they by the door?

## Slide 6

Look!
Here they are!
I found my shoes!

## Slide 7

I put on my shoes.
Now I’m ready!
Let’s go!
