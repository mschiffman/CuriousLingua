The rain has stopped.
The dark clouds are moving away.
Suddenly, a beautiful rainbow appears in the sky!
Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet!
What a wonderful sight!
But where do all these colors come from?
Let's find out!

A rainbow needs two important things.
It needs sunlight and tiny drops of water.
After a rainstorm, many raindrops may still be falling through the air.
When sunlight shines on these drops, something amazing can happen.
The sunlight can separate into beautiful colors!
But how?

Sunlight looks white to our eyes.
But it contains many different colors!
Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and violet are all part of sunlight.
Usually, these colors travel together.
That is why we see white light.
A raindrop can help separate them!

Imagine a tiny raindrop floating in the air.
A ray of sunlight enters the drop.
As the light moves from air into water, it bends.
Scientists call this bending refraction.
Different colors bend by different amounts.
The colors begin to separate!

What happens next?
Some of the light reflects off the back of the raindrop.
It travels back toward the front of the drop.
Then it leaves the water and enters the air again.
As it leaves, the light bends once more.
Now the different colors spread farther apart!

One raindrop is very small.
But there can be millions of raindrops in the sky!
Each drop sends colored light in different directions.
Only some of that light reaches our eyes.
Together, the light from many drops creates a beautiful rainbow.
What an amazing trick of nature!

Have you noticed that rainbows look like big arches?
There is a reason!
The colored light reaches our eyes from raindrops at particular angles.
These raindrops form a circle around a point in the sky opposite the Sun.
From the ground, we usually see only part of that circle.
That is why a rainbow looks like an arch!

Would you like to see a rainbow?
Look for sunshine while rain is falling in the distance.
Stand with the Sun behind you.
Look toward the raindrops in front of you.
You might see a beautiful rainbow!
Remember: sunlight bends, reflects, and separates into colors inside raindrops.
Now you know how a rainbow appears!
