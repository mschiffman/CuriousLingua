# At the Pharmacy

## 1. Before Leaving

**Lucas:** Emma… I think this is the end.

**Emma:** It's a cold, Lucas.

**Lucas:** My throat hurts, my head hurts, everything hurts.

**Emma:** I'm going to the pharmacy. What do you need?

**Lucas:** A miracle.

**Emma:** I'll ask if they have one without a prescription.

## 2. At the Counter

**Emma:** Hi, I'd like something for a cold, please.

**Pharmacist:** Sure. Is it for you?

**Emma:** No, it's for my husband.

**Pharmacist:** What are his symptoms?

**Emma:** He has a runny nose, he's coughing, and he complains a lot.

**Pharmacist:** Does he have a fever?

**Emma:** Ninety-nine. He says it's serious.

**Pharmacist:** Is he allergic to anything?

**Emma:** Housework. That's all.

## 3. The Treatment

**Pharmacist:** OK. This cough syrup: one spoonful in the morning and one at night.

**Emma:** Got it. And the tablets?

**Pharmacist:** One tablet three times a day, with meals.

**Emma:** For how long?

**Pharmacist:** Five days at most. If he doesn't feel better, he should see a doctor.

**Emma:** Do I need a prescription?

**Pharmacist:** No, it's over the counter. That'll be nine forty.

**Emma:** By card, please.

## 4. Back Home

**Lucas:** So? What did she say?

**Emma:** One tablet three times a day, and syrup morning and night.

**Lucas:** And how long do I have to stay in bed?

**Emma:** She said you can do the dishes tonight.

**Lucas:** She really said that?

**Emma:** It's written on the box.
