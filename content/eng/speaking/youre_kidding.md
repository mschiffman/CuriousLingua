# You're Kidding!

## The Ham

**Emma:** Lucas. Where's the ham?
**Lucas:** You're not going to believe this. It was the cat.
**Emma:** Yeah, right.
**Lucas:** I swear! He opened the fridge.
**Emma:** Give me a break. He can't even use his cat door.
**Lucas:** I saw it with my own eyes.
**Emma:** And you just watched?
**Lucas:** I was in shock.
**Emma:** As if! Did he put the plate in the dishwasher, too?
**Lucas:** He has very good manners.

## The Cleaning

**Emma:** I don't believe it! You vacuumed?
**Lucas:** And I washed the windows.
**Emma:** No way!
**Lucas:** It's true! Even the bathroom.
**Emma:** Unbelievable. What did you break?
**Lucas:** Nothing! Believe me.
**Emma:** Lucas.
**Lucas:** The blue vase. But look how the floor shines.

## The Race

**Emma:** I signed us up for a race on Sunday.
**Lucas:** You're joking, right?
**Emma:** Not at all. It starts at eight.
**Lucas:** Are you kidding me? On a Sunday?
**Emma:** It'll be good for us.
**Lucas:** You can't be serious. We never run! How far is it?
**Emma:** One mile. It's the family fun run.
**Lucas:** For real? One mile?
**Emma:** And there's a chocolate medal at the finish line.
**Lucas:** You should have started with that.

## The Lottery

**Lucas:** Holy cow! Emma! We won the lottery!
**Emma:** What? That's impossible!
**Lucas:** It's true! Look at the numbers!
**Emma:** That's crazy! How much?
**Lucas:** Hold on… Six dollars.
**Emma:** Six dollars.
**Lucas:** That's insane, right?
**Emma:** Unbelievable. And how much did you spend on tickets?
**Lucas:** Ten. But we still won.
