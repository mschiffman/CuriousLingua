# What Do You Think?

## The Painting

**Lucas:** Look. I hung the painting.
**Emma:** Oh. Yes. I see.
**Lucas:** What do you think?
**Emma:** Honestly?
**Lucas:** Honestly.
**Emma:** I think it's… interesting.
**Lucas:** "Interesting" is not a compliment.
**Emma:** In my opinion, it's upside down.
**Lucas:** No, it isn't. It's abstract art.
**Emma:** Lucas, the signature is at the top.

## The Soup

**Emma:** So, how do you like my soup?
**Lucas:** It's… different.
**Emma:** Different?
**Lucas:** I feel like something is missing.
**Emma:** Salt?
**Lucas:** No, I don't think so.
**Emma:** Pepper?
**Lucas:** Maybe. I'm not sure.
**Emma:** So what's missing, in your opinion?
**Lucas:** The phone number of the pizza place.

## The Movie

**Lucas:** What are we watching tonight?
**Emma:** There are two movies. Which one is better, in your opinion?
**Lucas:** The first one. No question.
**Emma:** Are you sure?
**Lucas:** Absolutely. If you ask me, it's a masterpiece.
**Emma:** Perfect. We'll watch the second one.
**Lucas:** What? But you asked for my opinion!
**Emma:** I did. So I'd know which one to avoid.

## The Mustache

**Lucas:** So? What do you think of my mustache?
**Emma:** I think it's brave.
**Lucas:** Personally, I think it makes me look serious.
**Emma:** Do you want my honest opinion?
**Lucas:** Yes.
**Emma:** It makes you look like your uncle Gerald.
**Lucas:** I like my uncle Gerald.
**Emma:** So do I. But I didn't marry him.
