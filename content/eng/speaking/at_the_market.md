# At the Market

## The List

**Emma:** So, did you go to the market?
**Lucas:** Yes. Everything's in the basket.
**Emma:** A pound of tomatoes?
**Lucas:** A pound. Nice and ripe.
**Emma:** A bunch of parsley?
**Lucas:** Cilantro. Same thing.
**Emma:** It's not the same thing at all. And the piece of cheddar?
**Lucas:** There's a piece left.
**Emma:** What do you mean, "left"?
**Lucas:** I tried it before I bought it. And a little after.

## The Peaches

**Lucas:** Are these peaches ripe?
**Emma:** Stop touching everything.
**Lucas:** I'm just checking. This one's hard. So is this one.
**Emma:** Is it for today or for tomorrow?
**Lucas:** For tonight.
**Emma:** Then you need a really ripe one.
**Lucas:** This one's soft. Very soft.
**Emma:** Of course it is. You've been squeezing it for five minutes.
**Lucas:** So it's ripe.
**Emma:** It's bruised. And now it's ours.

## Too Expensive

**Emma:** Four dollars for a basket of strawberries! That's expensive.
**Lucas:** They're organic, local, and in season.
**Emma:** You're reading the sign.
**Lucas:** Hold on. I'm going to bargain.
**Emma:** People don't really do that here.
**Lucas:** Watch and learn. … There. Two baskets for eight dollars.
**Emma:** Lucas. That's the same price.
**Lucas:** Yes, but now we have two.
**Emma:** And four dollars less.

## Cash Only

**Emma:** That comes to twelve fifty.
**Lucas:** I'll pay by card.
**Emma:** He doesn't take cards. Cash only.
**Lucas:** Not even tap to pay?
**Emma:** Lucas. His scale is older than you are.
**Lucas:** Fine. I've got a fifty.
**Emma:** He can't break a fifty. Do you have anything smaller?
**Lucas:** I have two dollars… and a button.
**Emma:** Never mind. I'll pay. You owe me twelve fifty and a button.
