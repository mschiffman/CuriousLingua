# Not Feeling Well

## The Cold

**Lucas:** Emma, I don't feel well.
**Emma:** What's the matter?
**Lucas:** I have a sore throat. And a runny nose. And a cough.
**Emma:** So, a cold.
**Lucas:** I think it's more serious than that.
**Emma:** Do you have a fever?
**Lucas:** Thirty-seven point two.
**Emma:** That's not a fever. That's a person.
**Lucas:** Tell my mother I loved her.
**Emma:** Tell her yourself. She's calling at six.

## The Headache

**Emma:** I have a headache.
**Lucas:** Since when?
**Emma:** Since this morning.
**Lucas:** Have you tried drinking water?
**Emma:** Yes.
**Lucas:** Fresh air?
**Emma:** Yes.
**Lucas:** You should lie down in a quiet room.
**Emma:** Good idea. Which room will you be in?

## The Back

**Lucas:** Ow! My back!
**Emma:** What happened?
**Lucas:** I picked something up. Something heavy.
**Emma:** What was it?
**Lucas:** It's not important.
**Emma:** Lucas.
**Lucas:** A sock.
**Emma:** One sock?
**Lucas:** It was wet.
**Emma:** You should see a doctor.
**Lucas:** And tell him what?
**Emma:** The truth. He needs a laugh too.

## Feeling Better

**Emma:** How do you feel today?
**Lucas:** A little better. But I'm still very weak.
**Emma:** Did you take your medicine?
**Lucas:** Twice a day, after meals.
**Emma:** Good. Can you do the dishes?
**Lucas:** Oh no. I feel dizzy.
**Emma:** Too bad. Paul called. Football at four.
**Lucas:** What time is it?
**Emma:** Three thirty.
**Lucas:** It's a miracle. I feel much better.
**Emma:** Wonderful. The dishes take twenty minutes.
