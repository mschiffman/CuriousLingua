# Present Continuous

## On the Phone

**Emma:** Hello? What are you doing?
**Lucas:** I am working.
**Emma:** You are watching TV. I hear it.
**Lucas:** I am working. The TV is helping.

## The Rice

**Emma:** Lucas! The rice is burning!
**Lucas:** It is not burning. It is cooking.
**Emma:** Smoke is coming out.
**Lucas:** It is cooking fast.

## The Rain

**Emma:** Take an umbrella. It is raining.
**Lucas:** It isn't raining.
**Emma:** People are running.
**Lucas:** They aren't running. They are walking fast.
**Emma:** Lucas, you are getting wet.
**Lucas:** I'm not getting wet. I am washing my hair.

## The Night

**Lucas:** Are you sleeping?
**Emma:** Yes.
**Lucas:** Are you really sleeping?
**Emma:** Yes. I am sleeping and talking.
**Lucas:** What is the cat doing?
**Emma:** She is sleeping too. Learn from her.
