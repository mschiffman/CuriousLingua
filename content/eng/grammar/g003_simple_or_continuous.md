# Simple or Continuous?

## On the Phone

**Emma:** What are you doing?
**Lucas:** I cook dinner.
**Emma:** You cook dinner? Every day? Since when?
**Lucas:** No. Now. I am cooking dinner now.
**Emma:** Ah. And every day?
**Lucas:** Every day, I eat dinner.

## The Cat

**Emma:** Lucas, the cat is sitting on your laptop.
**Lucas:** She sits there every day.
**Emma:** She is typing now.
**Lucas:** She types every day too.
**Emma:** She is sending an email to your boss.
**Lucas:** Good. He likes her emails.

## The Coffee

**Emma:** What are you doing?
**Lucas:** I am thinking.
**Emma:** What do you want?
**Lucas:** I want coffee. I need coffee. I love coffee.
**Emma:** I know. You are drinking my coffee.

## The Car

**Emma:** You usually walk to work.
**Lucas:** Yes. I walk every day.
**Emma:** Why are you taking the car today?
**Lucas:** Because it is raining.
**Emma:** It isn't raining.
**Lucas:** It is raining somewhere.
