# Le chien sans maison

*Marie rencontre un petit chien tout seul* — English translation by slide

## Slide 1

- Marie lives in a small town.
- One morning, she goes to the bakery.
- In front of the door, she sees a small brown dog.

## Slide 2

- The dog is dirty, thin, and very calm.
- Marie goes into the bakery.
- When she comes out, the dog is still there.

## Slide 3

- He looks at her with his big black eyes.
- Marie says softly, "Hello, little dog."
- "Are you alone?"

## Slide 4

- Marie walks toward her house.
- The dog follows her.
- In the garden, Marie gives him water and a little bread.

## Slide 5

- The dog eats very quickly.
- Marie asks the neighbors,
- "Do you know this dog?"
- But no one knows him.

## Slide 6

- In the afternoon, Marie goes to the vet.
- The dog does not have a collar.
- He does not have a microchip either.

## Slide 7

- He does not have a family.
- Marie looks at the little dog.
- The little dog looks at Marie.
- She smiles.

## Slide 8

- "Now, you have a home," she says.
- The dog wags his tail.
- That evening, he sleeps in a basket, warm, near the door.
- For the first time in a long time, he is no longer alone.
