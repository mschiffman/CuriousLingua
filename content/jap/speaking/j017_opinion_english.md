# What Do You Think? (J17): English lines by slide
## Karē
Riku: How's the curry?
Emi: Mm, it's good.
Riku: Really?
Emi: Really. But it's a bit…
Riku: A bit?
Emi: I just thought it might be a little sweet.
Riku: I put apples in it.
Emi: How many?
Riku: Three.
Emi: Then I think it's a dessert.
## Shatsu
Riku: What do you think of this shirt?
Emi: Looks fine, doesn't it?
Riku: Is that a compliment?
Emi: I think it's not bad.
Riku: Not bad…
Emi: But I think I like yesterday's shirt better.
Riku: I wore this one yesterday too.
Emi: …Then it suited you better yesterday.
## Bangohan
Riku: What do you want for dinner?
Emi: Anything's fine.
Riku: Then how about ramen?
Emi: Ramen's a bit…
Riku: Sushi?
Emi: Hmm… I'm not sure.
Riku: Curry?
Emi: We had that yesterday.
Riku: You said anything was fine.
Emi: Yes. Anything but those.
## Eiga
Riku: How was the film yesterday?
Emi: I think it was good.
Riku: What did you like about it?
Emi: Um… the ending.
Riku: The ending? Emi, you were asleep.
Emi: I wasn't asleep.
Riku: I have a feeling I heard snoring.
Emi: I was just listening with my eyes closed.
Riku: So who did it?
Emi: …What do you think, Riku?
