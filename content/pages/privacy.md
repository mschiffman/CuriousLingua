# Privacy Policy

### The short version: no accounts, no advertising, no tracking. We only receive personal information when you choose to write to us.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information about you when you use CuriousLingua at **curiouslingua.com** ("the site", "we", "us"). CuriousLingua is a small, free language-learning site run by one person. If anything here is unclear, please ask through the [Contact page](contact.html).

## What we do not do

- There are no user accounts, and you never have to give your name or email address to use a lesson.
- There is no advertising on the site.
- We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not build profiles of visitors.
- We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

## Information you give us: the contact form

When you send a message through the [Contact page](contact.html), we receive the **name**, **email address** and **message** you type. That is all the form asks for.

- We use this information only to read your message and to reply to you.
- We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not pass your details to anyone else.
- The form is delivered to our email inbox by a form-forwarding service, [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com/privacy). Web3Forms handles your message on our behalf in order to deliver it, and may keep a copy for a limited time.
- Your message then stays in our email inbox for as long as we need it to answer you and keep a record of the conversation. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Please do not put sensitive information (such as passwords, health details or identity numbers) in a message.

## Information collected automatically

**Hosting.** The site is hosted by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). As with any website, when your browser asks for a page, the hosting company's servers handle technical details such as your IP address, the type of browser and device, the page requested and the time. Cloudflare uses this to deliver the site and to protect it from abuse. We do not use it to identify you.

**Audio and video.** The recordings and videos in the lessons are loaded from our own media storage at languages.rmlives.com, which is also provided by Cloudflare. The same technical details are handled there.

## Cookies and storage on your device

We do not set any cookies. Cloudflare may occasionally set a strictly necessary security cookie to tell real visitors from automated traffic; it is not used for advertising or tracking.

When you tick a lesson as "done", that tick is saved in your own browser (in what is called local storage) so that the site can show it to you next time. It stays on your device, it is never sent to us, and you can remove it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser.

## Content from other services

A few parts of the site rely on other companies. When you use them, that company receives your IP address and its own privacy policy applies.

- **Offline lessons and some extra materials** are kept on Google Drive. Following those links takes you to Google.
- **One interactive game** in the English course loads its lettering from Google Fonts.

Links to other websites are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for how those sites handle your information.

## Downloaded lessons

Lessons you download for offline use are ordinary files that open in your browser. They work without an internet connection and do not send us anything.

## Children

Some lessons are written for children. Children can use every lesson without giving any personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a child, please ask a parent, guardian or teacher to write to us for you rather than using the contact form yourself. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us through the [Contact page](contact.html) and we will delete it.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold from a message you sent, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights over your personal information. To make a request, write to us through the [Contact page](contact.html) and we will answer as soon as we can.

## Changes to this policy

If the site starts to work differently (for example, if a new feature is added), we will update this page and change the date at the top.
