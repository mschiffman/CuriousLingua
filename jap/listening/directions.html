Where is the station?

えきはどこですか

A traveller asks the way to the station. Listen for where to turn and how far it is.

How to use this lesson

  1. Press Play all with the text hidden. Who is speaking, and where are they?
  2. Listen again, then answer the questions under the dialogue.
  3. Tap the lines to show the text, and listen once more while reading.
  4. Shadow it: play a line, press Pause (or the space bar), say it aloud, then Resume.
  5. Take a role and say your lines in the pauses.
Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

Notes

  • [Place] wa doko desu ka = Where is [place]?
  • massugu = straight ahead. migi = right. hidari = left.
  • ... te kudasai = please do ...: itte kudasai (please go), magatte kudasai (please turn).
  • aruite gofun = five minutes on foot.
