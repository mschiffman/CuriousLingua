At the Pharmacy

でかけるまえに・カウンターで・くすりののみかた・ただいま

Riku has a cold and is sure it is the end, so Emi goes to the pharmacy for him: four short scenes. Listen for how she asks for medicine with … ga hoshii n desu ga, for the pharmacist's polite questions, and for how to say how often and how long to take something.

Notes

  • Asking at the pharmacy: kazegusuri ga hoshii n desu ga (I'd like some cold medicine).
  • Symptoms: nodo ga itai, atama ga itai; hanamizu ga deru, seki ga deru; netsu ga aru.
  • The pharmacist's polite questions: Donata ga nomaremasu ka? Donna shōjō desu ka? Netsu wa arimasu ka? Arerugī wa arimasu ka?
  • Medicine is drunk: kusuri o nomu. Ichi-nichi san-kai, shokugo ni ichi-jō: one tablet three times a day, after meals. Asa to yoru: morning and night.
  • Shohōsen: a prescription. Irimasu ka? (is it needed?) Iie, irimasen (no, it isn't).
  • Numbers: 37.2 is sanjūnana-do ni-bu; 1,200 yen is sen nihyaku en. Paying: kādo de, genkin de.
  • Reporting words: … to itte imasu (he says that…), casual … tte (they said…).
