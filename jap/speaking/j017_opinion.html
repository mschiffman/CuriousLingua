What Do You Think?

カレー・シャツ・ばんごはん・えいが

A curry with three apples in it, a shirt that looked better yesterday, a dinner where anything is fine except everything, and a film Emi may have slept through: four short scenes between Emi and Riku. Listen for dō? and dō omou? when they ask, for … to omou when they answer, and for chotto…, the sentence nobody finishes.

Notes

  • Asking: A, dō? (how is A?), dō omou? (what do you think?), dō datta? (how was it?), nani ga ii? (what would you like?).
  • Giving an opinion: … to omou. Oishii to omou, dezāto da to omou, yokatta to omou.
  • Softening: kana (I suppose, I wonder), … to omotte left unfinished, ii n ja nai? (looks fine, doesn't it?).
  • The polite no: A wa chotto… Nobody finishes the sentence. Dō kana is another one.
  • Liking one thing better: A no hō ga suki. Kinō no shatsu no hō ga suki.
  • A feeling, not a fact: … ki ga suru. Ibiki ga kikoeta ki ga suru.
  • Not choosing: nan demo ii (anything is fine). Sore igai nara: as long as it's something else.
