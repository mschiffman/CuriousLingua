¡Hola!

Four ways to say hello and goodbye

A phone call from the next room, an honest answer to 'how are you?', a trip to the store that may last until tomorrow, and a goodbye with seven aunts in the way: four short scenes between Emma and Lucas. Listen for which greeting fits which moment, and for the small words Mexico adds: ¿bueno?, ahorita, al rato.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Hello by the clock: buenos días until noon, buenas tardes until dark, buenas noches after dark and at bedtime. Hola works all day.
  • ¿Cómo estás? is a greeting more than a question. The expected answer is bien, gracias, ¿y tú?
  • ¿Bueno? is only for answering the phone, and only in Mexico.
  • Leaving: ya me voy (I'm leaving), ya vámonos (let's go), ahorita vengo (I'll be right back), no me tardo (I won't be long).
  • Goodbyes: nos vemos, nos vemos al rato, hasta luego, hasta mañana, cuídate.
  • Salúdame a tu mamá: say hi to your mom for me. The reply is de tu parte.
