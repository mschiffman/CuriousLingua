Me siento mal

Four scenes about small health problems

A cold, a phone call nobody should make, a pot of chicken broth that is always full and a cure that works on everything: four short scenes between Emma and Lucas, and Lucas's mother. Listen for me duele, for tengo with what you have, and for how you say you feel better.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Pain: me duele + one thing, me duelen + more than one. Me duele la cabeza, la garganta, la espalda. Me duelen los pies.
  • What you have goes with tener: tengo tos, tengo fiebre, tengo gripa.
  • Feeling: me siento mal, me siento un poco mejor, ya me siento muy bien. Estoy mareado, estoy débil.
  • Advice: deberías + infinitive. Deberías ir al doctor.
  • Medicine: tomar la medicina, dos veces al día, después de comer.
  • Mexican words: la gripa (a cold), me escurre la nariz (my nose is running), el doctor, en todos lados.
  • Dice que…: he or she says that… Mi mamá dice que no hace falta.
