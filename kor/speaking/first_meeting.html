Meeting someone

만나서 반가워요

Introduce yourself. Then take Min's part: press 'I am Min' and Play all.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Jeoneun [name]-yeyo / -ieyo = I am [name]. Use -yeyo after a vowel (Jisu-yeyo) and -ieyo after a consonant (Min-ieyo).
  • [Place]-eseo wasseoyo = I come from [place].
  • Jeodo = me too.
  • Hangungmal is written 한국말 (han-guk-mal): the k is said ng before m.
