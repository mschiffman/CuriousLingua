No Way!

Four ways to say you can't believe it

An angel expected in the garden, a lemon syrup that gets thinner in the sun, a dangerous belly button and a great debate between six-year-olds: four true stories from a childhood in Yangon, told by Emma. Listen for how Lucas shows he cannot believe it, and how Emma insists that it is true.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Really? Seriously? No kidding? show surprise and ask the speaker to go on.
  • You're kidding. You can't be serious. Are you kidding me? say: I think this is a joke.
  • That's impossible. No way! Unbelievable! say: this cannot be true.
  • That's crazy! and Holy cow! are informal, for a big surprise.
  • Yeah, right and Nonsense! say that you do not believe the other person at all.
  • To answer: I swear. I'm not lying. Believe me. And before a surprising story: You're not going to believe this.
  • The stories use the simple past: said, believed, drank, filled, stopped, argued, won.
