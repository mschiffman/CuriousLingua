Hey!

Hello, how are you, goodbye

A greeting meant for the cat, a car that will not run, a man who leaves for work three times and an urgent appointment in the bedroom: four short scenes between Emma and Lucas. Listen for how they say hello, ask how things are, and say goodbye.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Hey and Hi are informal. Hello is for everyone. Good morning and Good evening are more formal.
  • How's it going? and What's new? are friendly questions. Short answers are normal: Good. Not much.
  • See you + time says when you will meet again: See you tonight. See you soon. See you tomorrow.
  • See ya and Catch you later are very informal. Use them with friends.
  • Before you leave, say it first: I'm off. I'd better get going. Gotta run!
  • Gotta = (have) got to. Say it; do not write it in school work.
