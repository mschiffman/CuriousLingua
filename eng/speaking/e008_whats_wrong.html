Are You Okay?

Four ways to show concern

A sleepless night with a known cause, a bike accident and a very small dog, a dinner guest who does not arrive and a laptop that is 'resting': four short scenes between Emma and Lucas. Listen for how they ask what is wrong, and for the answers that say very little.

Notes

  • First questions: Are you okay? Are you all right? What's wrong? What's the matter?
  • What happened? asks about something that is finished: a fall, an accident, bad news.
  • You look + adjective says what you notice: You look tired. You look worried.
  • Are you sure? asks again when someone says I'm fine but does not look fine.
  • I'm worried. What if…? Do you think something happened? are for a person who is not there.
  • Short answers: I'm fine. It's nothing. Don't worry. When the worry is over: That's a relief.
