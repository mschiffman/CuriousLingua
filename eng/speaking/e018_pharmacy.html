At the Pharmacy

Asking for medicine and understanding how to take it

Lucas has a cold and is sure it is the end, so Emma goes to the pharmacy for him: four short scenes. Listen for how she asks for medicine, the questions the pharmacist asks, and how to say how often and how long to take something.

Notes

  • Asking at the pharmacy: I'd like something for a cold / a cough / a headache, please.
  • Symptoms: a runny nose, a cough, a fever, a sore throat. He's coughing. My head hurts.
  • The pharmacist's questions: Is it for you? What are his symptoms? Does he have a fever? Is he allergic to anything?
  • How to take it: one tablet three times a day, with meals; one spoonful in the morning and one at night; five days at most.
  • A prescription: a paper from the doctor. Over the counter: no prescription needed.
  • Paying: That'll be nine forty. By card, please. In cash, please.
  • Fever in Fahrenheit: 98.6 °F is normal (37 °C). 99 °F is 37.2 °C.
