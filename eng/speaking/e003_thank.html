Notes

  • Thank you is for everyone. Thanks and Thanks a lot are friendly. Thank you so much is stronger.
  • Thanks for + noun or verb-ing: Thanks for your help. Thank you for coming.
  • To answer: You're welcome. My pleasure. Anytime. Don't mention it.
  • No, thank you says no politely. I'm fine, thanks means you do not need anything.
  • When you get a present: You shouldn't have! It means you are very kind.
  • Thanks again comes at the end, when you have already said thank you.
