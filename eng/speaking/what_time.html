What Time Is It?

Four conversations about the time

Emma asks the time at a bus stop, plans a film with Lucas, tells him he is late for class, and Lucas buys a train ticket from an agent. Listen for the time in each conversation, then say it with them.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • What time is it? / Do you have the time? ask for the time now. The answer starts with It's: It's ten to nine.
  • What time does the film start? asks when something happens. The answer uses at: At half past eight.
  • past = after the hour: twenty past ten (10:20), ten past two (2:10).
  • to = before the next hour: quarter to eight (7:45), ten to nine (8:50).
  • You can also say the numbers: ten fifteen (10:15), two thirty-five (2:35), four forty (4:40).
  • o'clock is only for the exact hour: nine o'clock, but half past nine.
