Well Done!

Four ways to say congratulations

A light bulb changed after three months, a driver's license passed on the fifth try, a pancake that does not come back down and a new job to celebrate: four short scenes between Emma and Lucas. Listen for how they congratulate each other, and for the moments when they do not really mean it.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Well done!, Nice work! and Congratulations! praise a success. Congrats is short and informal.
  • Congratulations on + noun: Congrats on the new job. Congratulations on your wedding.
  • You earned it and All that hard work paid off praise the work, not only the result.
  • You nailed it!, You've got this! and Way to go! are informal. Use them with friends.
  • I'm proud of you and I'm so happy for you show your feelings.
  • Tone can change the meaning: Way to go, said flatly after a mistake, is not praise.
