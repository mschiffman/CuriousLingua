I Mean…

Four ways to explain yourself

A strange cake, a sweater that got smaller, a plant that is 'resting' and a couch with a spring sticking out: four short scenes between Emma and Lucas. Listen for the little phrases they use to correct themselves, explain again and buy time.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • I mean corrects or sharpens what you just said. Or rather gives a more exact word.
  • Actually gives the real fact, or the real question. It does not mean 'now'.
  • In other words says it again plainly. What I'm trying to say is… tries again when the first attempt failed.
  • How do I put this? and Let's just say… buy time before bad news.
  • Sort of is a careful yes: partly true.
  • Like, well and still are spoken words. Do not use like this way in writing.
