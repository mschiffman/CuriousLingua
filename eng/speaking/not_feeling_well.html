Not Feeling Well

Four scenes about small health problems

A cold that might be the end, a headache that needs a quiet room, a back hurt by a sock and a recovery timed to the minute: four short scenes between Emma and Lucas. Listen for I have a…, for the advice with you should, and for how you say that you feel better.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Sickness takes a: I have a cold, a fever, a cough, a headache, a sore throat, a runny nose.
  • Feeling: I don't feel well, I feel dizzy, I feel weak, I feel a little better, I feel much better.
  • Pain: my back hurts, my leg hurts. Or with -ache: a headache, a stomachache, a toothache.
  • Advice: you should + verb. You should lie down. You should see a doctor. Softer: Have you tried drinking water?
  • Medicine: take your medicine once a day, twice a day, three times a day, after meals.
  • Temperature: 37.2 is thirty-seven point two. In Celsius, about 37 is normal and 38 is a fever.
