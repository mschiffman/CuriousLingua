You're Kidding!

Four more ways to say you can't believe it

A cat accused of stealing the ham, a suspiciously clean apartment, a Sunday race and a lottery win: four short scenes between Emma and Lucas. Listen for the difference between 'I'm amazed' and 'I don't believe a word of it', and for how the other one insists.

Notes

  • Real surprise: I don't believe it!, No way!, Unbelievable!, That's impossible!, That's crazy!, Holy cow!
  • Checking that it is not a joke: You're joking, right?, Are you kidding me?, You can't be serious, For real?
  • Not believing a word: Yeah, right, Give me a break, As if! The voice is slow and flat.
  • Insisting: I swear!, It's true!, Believe me, I saw it with my own eyes.
  • Tone changes the meaning: Unbelievable! can be real surprise or sarcasm.
  • You're not going to believe this comes first, before the surprising news.
