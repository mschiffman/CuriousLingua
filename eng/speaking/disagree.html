I Disagree!

Four arguments, from polite to firm

The heating, a suitcase with six pairs of shoes, some missing chocolate and pineapple on pizza: four small arguments between Emma and Lucas. Listen for how the disagreement goes from polite to firm, and how they end it.

Role play: I am Press Play all. Say your lines in the pauses.

The audio for this lesson is not recorded yet.

Notes

  • Polite: I wouldn't say that, I don't quite agree, That's debatable.
  • Yeah, but… and I get it, but… accept the point first, then give the objection.
  • Firm: Not at all!, That's not true, You're wrong. They are direct: use them with friends and family.
  • That's beside the point rejects an argument that does not answer the question.
  • To end an argument: To each their own, We'll have to agree to disagree, Let's not argue about it.
  • I beg to differ and With all due respect are formal. Friends use them as a joke.
