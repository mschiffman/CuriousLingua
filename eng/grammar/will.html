The future with will

Decisions now, promises and what you think will happen

Use will when you decide at the moment of speaking, when you promise or offer, and when you say what you think will happen. For a plan you already have, use be going to.

When to use itHow to make itSignal wordsNegative: will notQuestionsWill or going to?

When to use it

Decisions now

You decide while you are speaking

  • I'm thirsty. I will get some water.

    No plan: the idea is new.

  • It's hot. I will open the window.

  • The phone is ringing. I will answer it.

  • Wait here. I will ask my mother.

Promises

You give your word

  • I will call you tonight.

  • I will never forget you.

  • We will be there at six.

  • I won't tell anyone.

  • I will always help you.

Offers and requests

Helping, and asking for help

  • That bag looks heavy. I will carry it.

  • I will show you the way.

  • Will you help me, please?

  • Will you open the door?

What you think

After I think, I hope, maybe, probably

  • I think it will rain tomorrow.

  • She will probably be late.

  • I hope you will like it.

  • Maybe he will come later.

  • One day people will live on the moon.

How to make it

Pattern

will + verb (the same for every subject)

Full formShort formNegative
I will goI'll goI won't go
she will goshe'll goshe won't go
they will gothey'll gothey won't go

Will never changes and the verb after it never changes: he will go, not he wills go, not he will goes, not he will to go.

If and when

After if and when, use the present simple, not will. The other part of the sentence takes will.

  • If it rains, we will stay at home.

    Not: If it will rain…

  • When I get home, I will call you.

  • If you study, you will pass the test.

  • I will tell her when I see her.

Signal words

tomorrow, next week / year, soon, later, one day, in the future, in 2050, I think, I hope, I'm sure, maybe, probably, I promise …

Negative: will not

will not (won't) + verb

  • I won't be late.

  • She won't come today.

  • Don't worry, it won't hurt.

  • They won't believe you.

Questions

Will + subject + verb ?

  • Will you come with us?

    Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

  • Will it be hot tomorrow?

  • When will they arrive?

  • What will you do?

  • Who will win?

Will or going to?

  • Will: decided now. “We have no rice.” “Oh! I will buy some.”

    I did not know before.

  • Going to: decided before. “We have no rice.” “I know. I am going to buy some.”

    It was already my plan.

  • Will: what I think. I think our team will win.

    My opinion.

  • Going to: what I can see. It is 3–0. Our team is going to win.

    The score tells me.
