The past continuous

What was going on at a moment in the past

Use it for an action that was in progress at a time in the past: it had started, and it was not finished yet. It is the background of a story. The past simple tells what happened.

When to use itHow to make itSignal wordsNegative: wasn't / weren'tQuestionsPast continuous or past simple?

When to use it

At a moment in the past

In the middle of an action

  • At eight o'clock I was eating breakfast.

  • This time yesterday we were sitting in class.

  • What were you doing at ten last night?

  • At midnight everyone was sleeping.

Interrupted action

Past continuous (long) + past simple (short)

  • I was sleeping when you called.

  • She was walking home when it started to rain.

  • We were watching TV when the lights went out.

  • He was running when he fell.

  • While I was cooking, the phone rang.

    While + the long action. When + the short action.

Background

The scene at the start of a story

  • The sun was shining and the birds were singing.

  • It was raining and the wind was blowing.

  • People were talking and children were running everywhere.

  • She was wearing a blue dress.

Two actions at the same time

While …

  • While I was reading, my sister was drawing.

  • Mother was cooking while father was washing the car.

  • They were laughing while we were working.

How to make it

Pattern

was / were + verb + -ing

Subjectbeverb + -ing
I / he / she / itwasplaying
you / we / theywereplaying

The -ing spelling is the same as in the present continuous: making, running, sitting, lying.

Verbs that stay simple

The thinking and feeling verbs do not take -ing in the past either: know, like, love, want, need, understand, believe.

  • I knew the answer.

    Not: I was knowing the answer.

  • She wanted to go home.

    Not: She was wanting…

Signal words

when, while, as, at that moment, at eight o'clock, this time yesterday, all day, all night, all morning …

Negative: wasn't / weren't

was / were + not + verb-ing

  • I wasn't listening.

  • He wasn't wearing a hat.

  • They weren't working when I arrived.

  • It wasn't raining when we left.

Questions

Was / Were + subject + verb-ing ?

  • Were you sleeping?

    Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.

  • What were you doing when I called?

  • Was it raining?

  • Where was she going?

  • Why were they laughing?

Past continuous or past simple?

  • Past continuous: in progress, not finished. I was reading a book.

    We do not know if I finished it.

  • Past simple: finished. I read a book.

    From the first page to the last.

  • Together: I was reading when she came in.

    The reading is the long action. Her coming in is the short one.

  • Two short actions: When she came in, I stood up.

    First she came in, then I stood up: both past simple.
