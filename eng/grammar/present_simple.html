The present simple

Habits, facts and things that are always true

Use it for things you do again and again, for facts, and for what you like, know and want. It is not about this minute: it is about every day, every week, always.

When to use itHow to make itSignal wordsNegative: do not / does notQuestions: do / doesPresent simple or present continuous?

When to use it

Habits

Things you do again and again

  • I get up at six every day.

  • She walks to school.

  • We play football on Saturdays.

  • My father drinks tea in the morning.

  • They often visit their grandmother.

Facts

Things that are always true

  • The sun rises in the east.

  • Water boils at 100 degrees.

  • Cats like fish.

  • Rice grows in water.

  • Yangon is a big city.

Likes and thoughts

What you like, know, want, think

  • I like mangoes.

  • He knows the answer.

  • We want to go home.

  • She loves her little brother.

  • I think it is a good idea.

Timetables

Fixed times: buses, lessons, shops

  • The bus leaves at eight o'clock.

  • School starts on Monday.

  • The shop opens at nine.

  • The film begins at seven tonight.

How to make it

Pattern

I / you / we / they + verb · he / she / it + verb + s

Subjectplaygohavebe
Iplaygohaveam
you / we / theyplaygohaveare
he / she / itplaysgoeshasis

Only he, she and it change the verb. Remember the s: she plays, not she play.

Spelling of he / she / it

  • Most verbs: add -s
    work → works, read → reads, live → lives.
  • After -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: add -es
    watch → watches, wash → washes, go → goes, do → does.
  • Consonant + y: change y to -ies
    study → studies, carry → carries, cry → cries.
  • Vowel + y: just add -s
    play → plays, buy → buys.
  • have → has. be → am, is, are.

Signal words

always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day / week / year, on Mondays, in the morning, at night, once a week, twice a month …

always, usually, often, sometimes, never go before the verb, but after am, is, are: I always walk to school. He is never late.

Negative: do not / does not

do not (don't) / does not (doesn't) + verb

  • I don't like coffee.

  • We don't have a car.

  • She doesn't eat meat.

  • He doesn't live here.

  • My sister isn't at home.

    With be, just add not: am not, isn't, aren't.

After doesn't the verb has no s: she doesn't like, not she doesn't likes.

Questions: do / does

Do / Does + subject + verb ?

  • Do you like mangoes?

  • Does she play the piano?

    Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

  • Where do they live?

  • What time does school start?

  • Is he your brother?

    With be, put am, is, are first.

Present simple or present continuous?

  • Present simple: every day, always. I walk to school.

    That is my habit.

  • Present continuous: right now. I am walking to school.

    I am on the road at this moment.

  • Together: He usually drinks tea, but today he is drinking milk.

    Usually = simple. Today, now = continuous.
