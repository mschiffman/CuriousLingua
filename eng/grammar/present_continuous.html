The present continuous

What is happening right now

Use it for something that is happening now, or around now, and is not finished. You can also use it for a plan with a time: I am meeting Su tomorrow.

When to use itHow to make itSignal wordsNegative: am not / isn't / aren'tQuestionsPresent continuous or present simple?

When to use it

Right now

It is happening at this moment

  • I am reading a book.

  • Look! It is raining.

  • The children are playing outside.

  • Be quiet. The baby is sleeping.

  • What are you doing?

Around now

These days, this week, this year

  • I am learning English this year.

  • She is staying with her aunt this week.

  • We are reading a story about a tiger in class.

  • My brother is looking for a job.

Changes

Things that are slowly changing

  • The days are getting longer.

  • Your English is getting better.

  • The baby is growing fast.

  • Prices are going up.

Plans

A fixed plan, with a time

  • I am meeting Su at five.

  • We are visiting our grandparents on Sunday.

  • He is flying to Bangkok tomorrow.

  • What are you doing this weekend?

How to make it

Pattern

am / is / are + verb + -ing

Subjectbeverb + -ingShort form
IamplayingI'm playing
he / she / itisplayingshe's playing
you / we / theyareplayingthey're playing

You need both parts: She is playing, not She playing and not She is play.

Spelling of -ing

  • Most verbs: add -ing
    play → playing, read → reading, go → going.
  • Verb ends in -e: drop the e
    make → making, write → writing, come → coming.
  • Short verb, one vowel + one consonant: double the last letter
    run → running, sit → sitting, swim → swimming, stop → stopping.
  • Verb ends in -ie: change to -ying
    lie → lying, die → dying.

Verbs that stay simple

Some verbs are about thinking and feeling, not doing. They do not usually take -ing:
like, love, hate, want, need, know, understand, believe, remember, have (= own).

  • I know the answer.

    Not: I am knowing the answer.

  • She wants some water.

    Not: She is wanting some water.

  • We have a dog.

    Not: We are having a dog.

Signal words

now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, this year, these days, Look!, Listen!, still …

Negative: am not / isn't / aren't

am / is / are + not + verb-ing

  • I am not watching TV.

  • He isn't listening.

  • They aren't playing today.

  • It isn't raining now.

Questions

Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing ?

  • Are you listening?

    Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

  • Is she coming with us?

  • What are they doing?

  • Why is the baby crying?

  • Where are you going?

Present continuous or present simple?

  • Present continuous: now, not finished. She is cooking dinner.

    She is in the kitchen at this moment.

  • Present simple: every day, always. She cooks dinner every evening.

    That is her routine.

  • Together: I usually walk, but today I am taking the bus.

    Usually = simple. Today = continuous.
